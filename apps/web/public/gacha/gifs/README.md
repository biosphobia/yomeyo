Reaction pictures handed out by the gacha go here.

Gif, animated webp, avif, png and jpeg all work — an animation or a
photograph, any size, any shape. Upload the original: every deploy squeezes
its own copy to the same budget (longest side 320px, 260 KB) and leaves what
is here untouched. Photographs are turned the right way up and have their
metadata — including where they were taken — dropped on the way out.

See docs/gacha.md — add the file, then add an entry to ../prizes.json.
